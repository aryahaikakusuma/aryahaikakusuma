On the gap

27 Oct 2026 · sample post, replace with your own

The picture on the home page is one painting shown twice. Adam’s hand, on the left, is the fresco itself, cut out of its wall. God’s hand, on the right, is the same painting turned into characters.From Michelangelo’s The Creation of Adam, Sistine Chapel, 1508–12, via Wikimedia Commons. The painting is in the public domain.

Between them there is a gap that does not close. When the cursor comes near, the right hand slides one character to the left and a small red mark in the gap brightens, but the fingers stay apart.The red is the only accent on the site. Scribes used it for initials, and here it marks the first letter of a post, the page numeral in the navigation, and the spark.

The ASCII half is shaded with a ramp of fourteen characters, from a light full stop to a heavy at sign. The characters carry the tone, so the hand is drawn with the same marks as the text around it.

The rest of the site follows the same rule: two voices, a serif for reading and a monospace for everything that is data, and no more colour than the page needs.

Set in EB Garamond and Fragment Mono.
